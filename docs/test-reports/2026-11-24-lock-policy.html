By hand, on the real screens

One test server, one Server PC window, one student PC window. Class 7, teacher Ravi K, student Asha Patil.

1

Turns the switch on

Super Admin

Signed in as admin on the Server PC, opened System, ticked "Lock the lab PCs when no class is booked in the lab" and pressed Save. Class 7 is booked today in Period 1, which is already over.

Passed. The switch shows, ticks, and saves ("Saved.").

2

Before the switch is saved

Student PC

Lab PC 2 is waiting on its sign-in screen. Nobody has locked it.

Passed. The PC is open.

3

After the switch is saved

Student PC

Nobody pressed Lock. No class is in the lab right now, so the server locked the PC by itself.

Passed. Covered by "Look at your teacher" within 15 seconds (it took about 9).

4

Sees the lab is locked

Teacher

Signed in as Ravi K (teacher of Class 7). His menu has no System item, so he cannot reach the switch. Under School → Devices he sees Lab PC 2 marked "Screen locked".

Passed. No System menu for a teacher. A direct try to change the settings is refused (HTTP 403).

5

Unlocks by hand

Teacher

Ravi pressed Unlock all screens, for example for an extra class.

Passed. The "Screen locked" mark is gone and the student PC opened at once.

6

Signs in and works

Student

Asha Patil (Class 7, roll 1) signed in on Lab PC 2. I waited more than 30 seconds after the teacher's unlock.

Passed. The PC stayed open. The teacher's unlock holds.

7

A period ends while she is signed in

Student

The Super Admin changed Period 1 to be running now (the PC stayed open), then to have ended.

Passed. Asha's screen was covered within 17 seconds, while she was signed in.
